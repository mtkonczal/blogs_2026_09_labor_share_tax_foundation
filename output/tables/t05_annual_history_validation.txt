level. The only exceptions are 1948 (immediate postwar reconversion) and a
handful of years right next to the current one (2012's brief profit-margin
peak, and the 2021-23 post-pandemic whipsaw), none of which is more than 1.3pp
below 2025. From 1951 on, the gap to 2025 is at least +1pp every single year
through 2020; from 1953-2009 (with the exception of 2010-12) it is at least +2pp.
This is not a peak-vs-trough comparison: using ANY single year from 1949-2020 as
the starting point, not just the 1970 peak, shows a decline to today.
